WHAT IT DOES

From raw sources to
reusable knowledge

The Agent prioritizes content quality and verifiability, rather than just producing a quick summary.

01 / TRANSCRIBE

Responsible transcription

Provide separate verbatim and edited transcripts. Flag uncertain names, figures, or audio with timestamps for review.

02 / UNDERSTAND

Summary theo mục tiêu

Distinguish main points, essential context, and supporting details; retain figures or examples when they substantiate key ideas.

03 / EXTRACT

Extract noteworthy insights

Organize insights by topic and importance, merge duplicates, and cite quotes and information that needs verification.

04 / VOCABULARY

Build an English–Vietnamese glossary

Prioritize new terms, specialist language, and easily mistranslated phrases; explain them in project context with concise bilingual examples.

05 / APPLY

Suggest practical applications

Assess feasibility, fit for the target audience, and likely outcomes before suggesting a test.

06 / TRACE

Preserve verification trails

Record sources, dates, and timestamps when available; never present unverified claims as facts.

HOW IT THINKS

Workflow rõ ràng,
with clear checkpoints

Every step is guided by project goals and evidence in the original source.

Screen

Confirm approval and verify that the content genuinely serves the project.

Record sources

Distinguish the publication date, date received, and forwarding contact.

Transcribe & review

Compare against the audio; flag unclear passages with timestamps.

Synthesize

Create contextual summaries, key insights, and a glossary.

Verify

Recheck sources and flag items that need human verification before use.

QUALITY & BOUNDARIES

Trustworthy by being clear
about what is unknown

The Agent supports research and synthesis. People remain in control of important decisions and how content is used.

✓ Quality standards

  • Prioritize accuracy and original meaning.
  • Cross-check figures, proper names, terminology, and flagged passages.
  • Include sources or timestamps for verification.
  • Distinguish facts, speaker opinions, and hypotheses.
  • Preserve originals; create separate edited copies when authorized.

⦸ What it will not do

  • Invent facts, quotes, or sources.
  • Accept material based on its title alone; approval must be confirmed.
  • Delete, overwrite, edit, or move original files without authorization.
  • Publish, send, share, or disclose content on its own.
  • Claim a task was completed without evidence of a successful run.
The Agent pauses and asks for human input when judgment is needed

Audio is unclear; sources conflict; names or figures are unverified; approval is uncertain; or a choice could affect data, privacy, or other people.
